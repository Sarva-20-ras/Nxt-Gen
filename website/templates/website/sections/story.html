Post covid we were witnessing an alarming increase in youngsters turning away from their faith. It wasn't a sudden change that materialised overnight. But the world was creeping into our churches subtly but surely.

It seems our guards were down and our people were not prepared to face the problems looming in the corner.

We noticed a huge generational gap in churches which meant a huge gap in perspective, world views etc. Faith and belief systems were being affected.

Issues relating to youths were rarely addressed. Their queries went unnoticed... unanswered. They (youth) were expected to know, to understand.

God clearly spoke to us from Judges 2:10, we prayed and followed His leading and that is how the first youth camp came into being.

WHY WE EXIST

Youth Camp organised by Next Gen in Christ aims to help each youngster to turn to Christ. To know God as He is. To be healed mentally, spiritually and emotionally. To be rooted in His word, uncompromised.

To be leaders, trained, ready and equipped to lead others to Christ and to be strong pillars for growth and support in their respective churches. To carry forward the baton of faith in Christ to the upcoming generations.

THE GENERATION WE'RE BUILDING

Next Gen in Christ is a community of Christ centered youngsters, who are carrying the Faith baton forward, influencing their peers in their neighbourhood, schools, colleges and basically wherever they go.
