WHO WE ARE

WE'RE NOT JUST
ANOTHER YOUTH GROUP.

NextGen in Christ is a generation of young people choosing to follow Jesus, grow in His Word, live out their faith, and take the Gospel beyond the four walls.

We gather to worship, learn, build real friendships, serve one another, and become the people God has called us to be.
